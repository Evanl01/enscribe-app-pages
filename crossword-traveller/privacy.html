Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Overview

Crossword Traveller (“the App”) is published by Enscribe. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location for gameplay.

Game progress and in-app wallet/settings data are stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data stays on your device unless you delete the App or clear its data.

Network & third parties

The App does not send your gameplay progress to Enscribe servers. If advertising or analytics partners are added in a future update, this policy will be updated before those features collect data.

Children

The App is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@enscribe.online
