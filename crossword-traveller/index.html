Crossword Traveller

Support for the iOS word puzzle game.

How to play

  • Choose a region (chapter) from the home screen.
  • Open a puzzle and use the letter tiles to form words.
  • Find every word on the list to complete the puzzle.
  • Progress is saved on your device as you play.

Need help?

Email support@enscribe.online and include your device model and iOS version if something isn’t working.
